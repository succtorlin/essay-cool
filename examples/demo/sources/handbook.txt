Q: Where do you start on a span you have never seen?
A: You start underneath. Unlike many teams, Riverside begins at the bearings
and works upward, because a bearing that has seized tells you more about the
deck than the deck will tell you about the bearing. 1 have never once regretted
starting low.

Q: What do inspectors most often get wrong?
A: They photograph the damage and forget to photograph the scale. A crack
without a ruler beside it is an anecdote, not a measur ement.
