## SOURCE
Dana Okonkwo (former lead inspector, Riverside Transit Authority):
> "You start underneath."

On photographing damage, the same inspector:
> "A crack without a ruler beside it is an anecdote, not a measurement."

Priya Raman (former district engineer, Fairview County) on timing:
> "Longer than anyone budgets."

## METHOD
1. Begin at the bearings, work upward (#1).
2. Every damage photograph includes a scale reference (#2).
3. Key every photograph to a sketch (#3).

## LIMITS
Both accounts come from two inspectors at two authorities. Do not generalise
to spans or climates they did not work on. Neither gives a defect threshold.
